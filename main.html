Movie Summary

Spider-Man: Brand New Day follows Peter Parker as he continues his life as Spider-Man in a world where nobody remembers him. After sacrificing his personal relationships, Peter focuses entirely on protecting New York City. However, a new and powerful threat emerges, bringing unexpected challenges. As he struggles with loneliness and his responsibilities, Peter must discover what it truly means to be a hero.
